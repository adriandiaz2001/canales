Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Publicador canales ("the app") is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the channels Error 404: Neuronas and Bit a Bit to upload their own short educational videos to their own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok through the official APIs. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data the app accesses

  • Basic account information of the owner's own accounts (account ID, username, display name), only to check that each video goes to the correct account.
  • The permission to upload and publish videos on those accounts.

The app does not access followers, messages, comments, contacts, location, or any data about other people.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer, in a local file. They are never sent to any server other than the official API of the platform that issued them. No analytics, cookies or tracking are used.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use. Access can be revoked at any time from the security settings of each platform (Google account permissions, Instagram "Apps and websites", TikTok "Manage app permissions"), and the local files can be deleted. To request deletion or ask any question, write to error404neuronas@gmail.com (Error 404: Neuronas) · bitabitexplica@gmail.com (Bit a Bit).

YouTube uploads use the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Política de privacidad

Publicador canales es una herramienta privada de escritorio que usa el titular de los canales Error 404: Neuronas y Bit a Bit para subir sus propios vídeos divulgativos a sus propias cuentas de YouTube, Instagram y TikTok mediante las API oficiales. No se ofrece al público ni tiene otros usuarios.

  • Datos a los que accede: identificador y nombre de las cuentas propias, solo para comprobar que cada vídeo va a la cuenta correcta, y el permiso para subir y publicar vídeos. No accede a seguidores, mensajes, comentarios ni datos de terceros.
  • Almacenamiento: los tokens se guardan únicamente en el ordenador del titular y solo se envían a la API oficial que los emitió. Sin analítica ni cookies.
  • Cesión: no se venden ni ceden datos a terceros.
  • Supresión: el acceso puede revocarse en cualquier momento desde los ajustes de seguridad de cada plataforma. Para cualquier solicitud: error404neuronas@gmail.com (Error 404: Neuronas) · bitabitexplica@gmail.com (Bit a Bit).
